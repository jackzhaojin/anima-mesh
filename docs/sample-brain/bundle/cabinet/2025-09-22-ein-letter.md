# EIN assignment letter

The IRS EIN assignment letter. Original in the document store.

**The EIN itself is deliberately not recorded in this bundle.** A filing
that needs it must be prepared from the original letter, freshly read —
not from a concept that could drift, and not from model recall. What this
concept records instead:

- The letter exists, is filed in the document store, and was verified by
  the librarian 2026-03-18.
- The IRS-printed first return due date on the letter matches the
  calendar's Form 1120 row.

This is the pattern for any fact too dangerous to be wrong: record where
truth lives, not a copy of it.
