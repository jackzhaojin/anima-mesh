# Decision: one brain per boundary

**Decided 2026-10-30.** This is the one brain for the company. Every
project, product line, client pursuit, and side effort lives here as
folders and concepts. Large files stay in the company's drive (the file
cabinet), and the concept that needs them links to them.

A new brain is warranted only for a new boundary: a different person, a
different organization whose knowledge must stay with it, or a group of
people the work is shared with. The founder's own life is the founder's
person brain; a product team with its own members would be a team brain
under this one.

**Why.** Every brain carries its own constitution, roster, heartbeat,
secrets, and deploy. A second brain for the same owner doubles that
upkeep, splits the brief across two hubs, and turns every cross-project
question into a link to manage.

**Amendment.** Added to the [constitution](../../constitution.md) as the
standing rule "One brain per boundary," by the founder, with a log entry.
